What is Customer Lifetime Value?

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) estimates the total revenue a business can reasonably expect from a single customer account throughout the business relationship.

Our intelligent pipeline ingests your historical demographics and transactions, automatically computes rigorous RFM metrics (Recency, Frequency, Monetary), and runs them through our bespoke Random Forest Regressor to output high-accuracy future value estimations.
