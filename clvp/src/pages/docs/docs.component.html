Documentation & Rules

Learn how to use the Custom CSV Uploader and correctly format your three dataset files for the RFM & ML Pipeline.

How to use the ML Pipeline

  1. Prepare your three files: You need exactly three separate spreadsheets: Contact Data (Contact.txt), Transactions, and optionally a supplemental demographics file. Make sure they strictly follow the required column names below.
  2. Upload to Dashboard: Navigate to your Home page. Drop your Contact.txt file into the Contact Data box and your Transactions file into its box.
  3. Initialization: Click 'Start Custom CSV Upload'. The system will concurrently ingest rows in chunks into the Appwrite cloud database, including all contact fields and computed Loyalty Tier scores.
  4. Customer Sync View: Once the algorithm finishes uploading and computing Tenure, Average Order Value, and Loyalty Tier scores, navigate to the /customers portal to verify your computed variables before the Model Training step!

functions Engineered RFM Features

Here is exactly how our backend summarizes a customer's shopping habits, broken down into simple terms for the Machine Learning model:

1

Total Value

We look at everything the customer has ever bought and add up all the prices. This tells us the total amount of money they have spent with the business.

2

Transactions

We simply count how many separate receipts or orders they have attached to their name.

3

Avg Order

We take their Total Value and divide it by their number of Transactions. This tells us how much they usually spend on a typical shopping trip.

4

Last Purchase

We look through all the dates they have shopped, and pick out the most recent one (used for Recency).

5

Tenure

This measures how long they have been an active customer. We find the date of their very first purchase, and count the number of days between then and their last purchase. (If they've only ever shopped once in their life, we just count it as 1 day).

6

Frequency

This tells us how often they return to the store in a given month. We take their Tenure and convert it into months (e.g., 60 days becomes 2 months). Then, we divide their total transactions by those months.

7

Loyalty Tier Score

We map the customer's Loyalty Tier column from Contact.txt into a numeric score for the ML model: high = 3, medium = 2, low = 1, unknown = 0. This score is stored as loyalty_tier_score in the Features collection alongside the RFM metrics.
