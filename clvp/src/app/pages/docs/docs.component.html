How to use the ML Pipeline

  1. Prepare your CSV files: You need exactly two separate spreadsheets: One for Demographics and one for Transactions. Make sure they strictly follow the required column names below.
  2. Upload to Dashboard: Navigate to your Home page, and explicitly drop your respective Demographics file and Transactions file into their designated UI boxes.
  3. Initialization: Click 'Start Custom CSV Upload'. The system will concurrently ingest rows in chunks, storing them remotely into an Appwrite cloud database.
  4. Customer Sync View: Once the algorithm finishes uploading and executing dynamic Tenure & Average Order Value equations, navigate to the /customers portal to verify your computed variables before hitting the Model Training step!

group Demographics CSV Structure

Your demographics spreadsheet must contain the following columns exactly as written:

Column Name
Description
ContactId (Required) The unique primary identifier for the customer. Matches Transactions.

DateOfBirth Used for calculating demographic age bias nodes.

receipt_long Transactions CSV Structure

Each row represents a single purchase timeline element mapped to a customer:

Column Name
Description
ContactId (Required) Exact matching identifier pointing to Demographics.

functions Engineered RFM Features

Here is exactly how our backend summarizes a customer's shopping habits, broken down into simple terms for the Machine Learning model:

1

Total Value

We look at everything the customer has ever bought and add up all the prices. This tells us the total amount of money they have spent with the business.

2

Transactions

We simply count how many separate receipts or orders they have attached to their name.

3

Avg Order

We take their Total Value and divide it by their number of Transactions. This tells us how much they usually spend on a typical shopping trip.

4

Last Purchase

We look through all the dates they have shopped, and pick out the most recent one (used for Recency).

5

Tenure

This measures how long they have been an active customer. We find the date of their very first purchase, and count the number of days between then and their last purchase. (If they've only ever shopped once in their life, we just count it as 1 day).

6

Frequency

This tells us how often they return to the store in a given month. We take their Tenure and convert it into months (e.g., 60 days becomes 2 months). Then, we divide their total transactions by those months.
